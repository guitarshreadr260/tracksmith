Tracksmith GEN 1.1 - Drum Performance

TRACKSMITH GEN / Riff Lab

Hear it. Inspect the rhythm. Correct it. Build an editable drum part.

This is a rhythm-analysis experiment. It detects attack candidates, quiet spans and similar bar rhythms-not notes, chords or full riff meaning.

50% = straight. Swing shifts only offbeat cymbals. Kick/snare stay on the grid. Developing and Active vary the groove over four bars. Phrase markers restart the sequence.

Drum performance

Click a square to add or remove a hit. Right-click a hit to change its velocity. Changing controls or regenerating all replaces manual drum edits. Regenerating a bar restores its current generated pattern. After editing guitar markers, regenerate to apply them. Dragging depends on the browser and DAW; Export MIDI saves a file for import.

Select a bar or attack

Click empty space to add an attack, drag a dot to move it, or select it and Delete. Click a bar header to select that bar. Each bar shows its original audio position.
