TRACKSMITH GEN 1.1 - DRUM PERFORMANCE PROTOTYPE

Run ANALYZE-RIFF.cmd and choose your guitar WAV, tempo, first drum bar, and a NEW HTML filename. Open the resulting 1.1 page. You can also open DEMO-RiffLab-1.1.html to see the interface.

Adjust variation, guitar follow, energy, swing, cymbals and fills. Click squares in the Drum performance grid to add or remove hits; right-click a hit to change its velocity. Choose a bar in the dropdown. Save analysis + edits keeps your generated and edited drum notes. Export reviewed GGD drum MIDI writes the actual edited performance. Import at bar 1 at the displayed tempo, including the empty lead-in.

Changing generator controls or regenerating all replaces manual drum edits. Regenerate selected bar restores its current generated pattern; it does not create a random alternative. After changing guitar markers, regenerate drums to apply those changes. The grid uses the existing GGD One Kit Wonder Metal note mapping; MIDI mapping is not yet configurable. Swing can shift cymbal notes off the grid.

The drag button uses browser download drag support. Direct drops into Pro Tools and Tracksmith have not been tested and may not work; use Export MIDI and import the downloaded .mid if needed. The original WAV is not modified or uploaded. This remains a rule-based prototype, not a trained drummer or complete song-structure understanding.
