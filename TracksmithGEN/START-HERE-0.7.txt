TRACKSMITH GEN 0.7 / AUDIO-GUIDED VARIATIONS

QUICK START
1. Close your old GEN window.
2. Run UPDATE-GEN.cmd and choose the folder containing your existing GEN app.
   It backs up the old program files and leaves GEN-Library and settings alone.
   Or run this package's TracksmithGEN.exe separately with a new local library.
3. Open TracksmithGEN.exe. Click Import WAV / MP3 and choose your recording.
4. Choose a start time and a section from 5 to 30 seconds. Preview section
   prepares a comparison copy. Click A / Original to hear that section.
5. Describe the change. Set Variation amount (try 30-50%) and click Generate
   variation. A lower amount tends to retain more of the source; higher values
   allow more change. This control is the model's input noise level, not a
   guaranteed measure of musical similarity.
6. Click A / Original and B / Variation to compare. Switching preserves the
   playback position. Stop playback, then click A or B to play again. B plays
   the last completed variation; all generated takes appear in Your creations.
7. Export variation copies the WAV. Original section and variations are saved
   automatically in GEN-Library. Select any saved take -> Create variation to
   open the same audio-guided workflow on that take.

ENGINE
Text-only Multi Platinum still uses your existing ACE-Step installation.
Audio-guided variations use Stable Audio 3 Small Music in the separate working
sa3-guitar test environment you already installed. No reinstall is needed.
The app searches the existing extracted trial folders in Downloads. If that
environment was moved, use Model settings in the variation window to choose
its .venv\Scripts\python.exe. The worker uses cached assets offline and requires
CUDA; it does not silently download weights or fall back to CPU.

YOUR RECORDINGS
Mono/stereo WAV and MP3 are supported. The selected section is decoded to a
separate WAV. The original file is not changed. Source filename, section start,
variation amount, seed and reference-take link are retained in the saved library.
The model receives the selected audio, not just its filename or a prompt.
Models, credentials and your recordings are not included in this package.

LIMITS
This is audio-to-audio variation, not instrument isolation, stem extraction,
or bass/drum accompaniment generation. If you import a full mix, the full mix
is the reference. Tempo/key and note-for-note preservation are not guaranteed.
The imported reference is not peak-normalized, and A/B is not loudness-matched.
Preview fallback may be mono; stored/exported WAVs preserve stereo.
Audible quality is still for you to judge. Cancellation saves completed takes;
use Cancel job and allow it to stop before closing the window.
